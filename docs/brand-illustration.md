# Brand illustration

Tool: built-in `image_gen` (transparent background).

Project asset: `public/assets/brand-illustration.png`.

Role: a small editorial illustration beside the TraceScope wordmark. It is symbolic branding, not a clinical example, construction result or experimental visualization.

Style reference: `public/assets/composition.webp` (paper Figure 2). The prompt explicitly excludes reproducing its charts, numbers and topology.

## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one compact hero illustration for the TraceScope academic benchmark website. The attached paper figure is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: its friendly robot-and-stomach illustration, soft coral and teal palette, and fine dark outlines. Create a new, polished editorial illustration: a friendly small research robot stands beside a stylized coral upper-GI stomach, holding a magnifying glass toward three small floating translucent evidence-image tiles. The tiles contain only abstract teal and coral shapes. The robot and stomach are the main subjects, close together in a compact balanced square composition, with an elegant curved path connecting the tiles to the lens. Friendly scientific conference illustration, precise thin ink outlines, soft pastel shading with very subtle paper-like texture, powder blue, teal, coral, warm cream. Isolated on a genuinely transparent background, with only a faint soft contact shadow. Designed to look clean and legible at 180-240 pixels. No words, letters, labels, numbers, equations, charts, logos, hospital equipment clutter, realistic clinical imagery, gradients around the background, or watermark. Do not reproduce the reference figure's charts, topology, data or text.
